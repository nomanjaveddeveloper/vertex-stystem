Types of Viruses

Ransomware

Encrypts user data and demands payment for the decryption key.

Notable Example: WannaCry | Year: 2017 | Origin: Global/North Korea

Precautions: Regular offline backups, updated OS patches, and multi-factor authentication.

Trojan Horse

Disguises itself as legitimate software to gain unauthorized access to a system.

Notable Example: Emotet | Year: 2014 | Origin: Europe

Year-Wise Global Outbreaks

  • 1986: Brain - The first IBM PC virus. Origin: Lahore, Pakistan.
  • 1999: Melissa - Fast-spreading email worm. Origin: New Jersey, USA.
  • 2010: Stuxnet - Targeted industrial systems. Origin: Undisclosed.
